# Shared: how to give a critique

## 1. When to read this
Review mode, and any time it gives notes on someone's work (a brief from the Writer, drafts from the Artist or a human artist, an ad cut as frames).

## 2. Before the notes
1. **Agree the scope:** which files, against which brief and style guide, for which use (store icon, ad thumbnail, in-game, store screenshot). No brief or guide: say what it judged against instead, and mark the verdicts `inferred`.
2. **Judge the work, not the taste.** Each note ties to the brief, the guide, the use or honesty. "I do not like it" is not a note.

## 3. The checks
- **1-second, then 5-second read as a stranger:** what is it, who is it for, what is the one thing it says?
- **Squint test:** does it reduce to two or three clear shapes?
- **Small-size read:** from a small export only (see `what-it-can-see.md`).
- **Rules:** palette, shape language, line weight, light direction, detail level, as the guide states them.
- **Set consistency:** does each file follow the same rules as the others?
- **Honesty:** does the image show only what the game really has (see `honest-ads-and-rights.md`)?
- **Generic look:** a look any studio's AI tool would make, with no idea from this game, is a note.

## 4. Writing the notes
- Say what works first, briefly and specifically.
- Each problem: what, where in the image, which rule or goal it breaks, and the change that would meet it. The maker decides how to fix it.
- Ask a neutral question when the intent is unclear, instead of guessing.
- Verdict per file: **approve**, **fix** (with the exact note) or **redo** (with the reason).
- Severity per problem, the same scale as `hire-an-engineer/references/visual-quality.md`: 4 blocks the use, 3 major, 2 minor, 1 polish.

Note skeleton: `file | verdict | severity | what and where | rule or goal broken | change needed | opened: yes or no`

## 5. Limits of the review
- It is a gate before Art QA and the owner, never the final sign-off; this holds most of all for work it directed.
- It does not check file size, format, resolution, atlas fit, compression or platform specs; Art QA does.
- It does not rewrite the Writer's words; a note on words goes to the Writer through the boss.

## 6. Where these ideas come from (read live; checked to open on 2026-10-23)
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Design Critiques": https://www.nngroup.com/articles/design-critiques/ (clear scope, agreed objectives).
- Liz Lerman, Critical Response Process: https://lizlerman.com/critical-response-process/ (meaning first, neutral questions, opinions with permission).
- Ed Catmull, "How Pixar Fosters Collective Creativity" (HBR): https://hbr.org/2008/09/how-pixar-fosters-collective-creativity (notes on the work; the maker decides the fix).
These are summarised in its own words; the claims inside them were not all checked.
